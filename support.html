Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Wermom Premium → Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Wermom, go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Your premium access will activate automatically.

Is my data private?

Yes. All health data is encrypted on-device and synced via Supabase with row-level security. We never share data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your data within 30 days per our retention policy.

The 7-day free trial — how does it work?

New subscribers get 7 days free of Wermom Premium. Cancel anytime before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Manage in iPhone Settings → Subscriptions.

I lost my data after switching phones — help!

Don't worry. Sign in to Wermom on your new device with the same email. Your data is securely stored in the cloud and will sync automatically. Need help? Email us.

How do I report a bug?

Email hello@wermom.com with subject "Bug Report". Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and screenshots if possible.

Wermom AI gave wrong info — what should I do?

Wermom AI provides educational information based on WHO, AAP, and peer-reviewed research. It is NOT medical advice. For any health concern, please consult your doctor or pediatrician.
